# Trend Vault E-Commerce Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Executive Summary
An end-to-end e-commerce customer behavior and churn risk analysis evaluating 350 regional accounts across major U.S. markets.

## Key Insights Uncovered
* **Geographic Churn Isolation:** 100% of high-churn-risk accounts (116 total) were concentrated strictly in Chicago and Miami among Bronze/Silver members.
* **Discount Suppression:** Un-targeted blanket discounts reduced average spend by 12.9% ($787.27 vs. $903.49 non-discounted).
* **Revenue Concentration:** Gold tier members represent 33.4% of users but yield over 51% of gross revenue ($1,311.14 average spend).
